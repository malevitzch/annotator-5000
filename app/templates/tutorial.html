Basics

Use keys 1-4 to select/unselect labels, press enter to submit. The labels are as follows:

  • Edge of the splat - you can see edge of the scene and the void beyond it
  • Visible gaussians - single gaussians are visible too well, best seen on examples
  • Unrealistic blur - the image or a part of it is blurry (often due to a large gaussian)
  • Doesn't resemble a photo in any way - self-explanatory, you just don't know what you're even looking at
